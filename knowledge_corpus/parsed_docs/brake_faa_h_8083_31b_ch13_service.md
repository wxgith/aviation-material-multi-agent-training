                                                                   Air in the Brake System
                                                                   The presence of air in the brake system fluid causes the brake
                                                                   pedal to feel spongy. The air can be removed by bleeding to
                                                                   restore firm brake pedal feel. Brake systems must be bled
                                                                   according to manufacturers’ instructions. The method used
                                                                   is matched to the type of brake system. Brakes are bled by
                                                                   one of two methods: top down, gravity bleeding or bottom
                                                                   up pressure bleeding. Brakes are bled when the pedals feel
                                                                   spongy or whenever the brake system has been opened.
